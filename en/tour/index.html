Advisor account tour

What you will find in the Advisor Hub and AVRASTRO CRM before you create an account. The screenshots use fictitious data.

This page shows what exists today, without promises. We start with the Advisor Hub on avrastro.com and continue with three AVRASTRO CRM screens.

The Advisor Hub

Once you sign in with an advisor account, you see the verification status of your registration number and the AVRASTRO CRM card: trial or plan, renewal date, the “Open AVRASTRO CRM” button and billing management. Below it there are three sections:

  • Bookings: requests from citizens for times that are genuinely free. You confirm, decline or cancel them, with a note if you wish; the citizen is told by e-mail.
  • Messages: whatever citizens send from the contact form or as a call-back request.
  • Shares: tool results a citizen has sent you, the date they are available until, and whether they allow you to send them summaries.

From the Hub you also set your availability and public profile and build a client report to print.

AVRASTRO CRM: the Today screen

The first screen every morning: appointments, tasks, reminders and anything that needs attention, each with an explanation.

The client view

Everything about a client and their household: policies, cover, applications, documents, notes, reviews and next steps. Needs analysis, comparisons and proposals start from here.

Applications by stage

Every application with its stage, the documents still missing and who you are waiting for. When something stalls, you see it before your client has to tell you.

Your first day

The first time you open AVRASTRO CRM, a short guide helps you choose your language and market, complete your profile, upload a logo and set goals and reminders. You can skip it and replay the tour later from Settings. If you keep contacts in a spreadsheet, you import them as a list with a preview; if you used the previous version of the CRM, you import its file and see what will be transferred before anything is saved.

The people, policies and amounts in the screenshots are fictitious and do not belong to real clients.
