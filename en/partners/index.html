Partners and Local Circle listings

For insurers, clinics and local professionals who want to appear on AVRASTRO, on clear terms.

AVRASTRO is a place of knowledge and trust for families in Cyprus. We only take on partners who add real value for our readers.

Who can partner with us

  • Insurers, for educational guides and content clearly marked as sponsored.
  • Clinics and health professionals, for information and prevention.
  • Local professionals and businesses, for a Local Circle listing, when their work relates to family and financial planning.

Ways to work together

  • A sponsored guide or article, clearly labelled.
  • A partner page describing your services, with contact details.
  • A Local Circle listing.
  • Placement in relevant sections of the website, always labelled.

Our principles

  • All sponsored content and every listing is clearly labelled.
  • We only accept what relates to family protection, health and financial planning.
  • We do not sell or pass on users’ personal data to partners.
  • Partnerships do not affect the tools’ calculations or the views we express in our articles.
  • We may decline a partnership that does not fit AVRASTRO’s tone and values.
