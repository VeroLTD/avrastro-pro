From a client’s payslip to a proposal

Upload one or more payslip PDFs — or open those already in the client’s documents. AVRASTRO CRM reads the amounts on your device, checks that gross minus deductions equals net and works out the year’s tax under the Cyprus rules.

You see the room left for deductible premiums and the illustrative saving, and save them to the client’s file together with the version of the rules that was used.

  • Every amount shows the payslip line it was read from
  • 13th and 14th salary, provident fund, medical fund and union
  • Tax-saving opportunities across your book and on the Opportunity Radar
  • A pay rise or a new employer becomes a life event with a review reminder

Share with your client in one step

Choose what to send, see the message exactly as the client will receive it and send it from your own app. Every share is noted on the client’s timeline.

  • What you send: a summary, policies and premiums, the protection overview, the tax result, the next appointment, renewals.

Works offline

AVRASTRO CRM runs as a Windows and macOS app and as a web app. Your data lives on your device, so you keep working at a client’s home, while travelling or when the connection drops.

  • When you are back online, changes sync safely between your devices.
  • If the same record changed on two devices, you see the difference and decide.
  • Your licence is checked offline too, so the program opens without an internet connection.

Security and data ownership

  • Cloud backups are encrypted on your device with a password the platform never sees. Keep it safe: without it the backup cannot be opened.
  • Signed licences: if your subscription ends, your workspace becomes read-only. Your data is never deleted.
  • Exporting all of your data is always available, even after expiry.
  • Two-factor sign-in (TOTP) with an authenticator app.
  • For teams: seats, roles, invitations and an activity log.

To be precise: backups are end-to-end encrypted. Records that sync between your devices are encrypted in transit but are not end-to-end encrypted, so that team features and support can work.

Connected to avrastro.com

AVRASTRO CRM and the website talk to each other, and you always have the final say.

  • Website inbox: bookings, messages and citizen shares arrive in the CRM for review. Nothing becomes a client automatically.
  • Client portal: you send summaries only to clients who have allowed it.
  • Single sign-on: open AVRASTRO CRM from the Advisor Hub without a second password.
  • AI drafts, if you want them: texts and summaries that you always review before they are used.

Questions

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Your data is on your device and the program opens and works without a connection. When you reconnect, changes sync with your other devices; if the same record changed in two places, you decide which version stands.

How do I move my data from spreadsheets or the previous version of the CRM?

From spreadsheets: paste the rows or upload a CSV or Excel file as a contact list, map the columns and see a preview with possible duplicates before anything is saved. You turn a contact into a client when you decide to.

From the previous version of the CRM: import your file from the data and safety section. You see what will be transferred first, and the import can be rolled back.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your workspace becomes read-only. Your data is not deleted and you can export it whenever you like; subscribe again and you carry on where you left off. If a single payment fails, you have a grace period to sort it out.

Where is the data hosted?

The main copy of your data is on your own device. For sync, accounts and backups, hosting is planned with a provider inside the European Union, chosen by AVRASTRO. Backups are already encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. More in the Privacy notice.

Can my team use it?

Yes, with the Team/Agency plan (from 3 seats). You invite colleagues, assign roles, see the activity log and use your agency branding on every document.

Do I have to use AI?

No. AI drafts are optional and you always review them before they are used. All of AVRASTRO CRM works normally without them.
